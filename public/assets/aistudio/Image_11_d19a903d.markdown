## Brand & Style

This design system defines an Islamic banking corporate fintech experience merged with dynamic SME (UMKM) digital commerce and verifiable blockchain security. The visual mood evokes trust, ethical clarity (*amanah*), empowerment, and contemporary innovation. It serves micro, small, and medium enterprise owners, institutional partners, and conscious consumers who require transparent transactional workflows alongside approachable digital companionship.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modern** structure with **Warm Tactile Minimalism**:
- **Purity & Openness:** Crisp white surfaces paired with subtle cool gray backdrops cultivate breathing room, avoiding cluttered financial dashboards.
- **Vibrant Purpose:** Authoritative teal reflects stability and Islamic financial heritage, contrasted against warm saffron-orange accents representing prosperity, active trading, and dynamic energy.
- **Modern Verification:** Cryptographic reliability and digital authenticity (B-CHAIN) are expressed through precise pill containers, luminous verification rings, and pristine micro-borders rather than cold technical clutter.
- **Friendly Professionalism:** Soft geometric curvature (`rounded-2xl`, generous touch surfaces) and empathetic illustration integration humanize financial governance into an empowering, daily business companion.

## Colors

The palette balances Islamic institutional credibility with the high-conversion needs of modern mobile commerce.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Teal (`#00A39D`):** The foundational anchor representing stability, digital finance, and ethical banking principles. Used for primary CTAs, active tab indicators, major header titles, and verified security emblems.
- **Secondary Orange (`#F8AD3C`):** High-energy saffron tone signifying merchant growth, promotion, notifications, attention flags, and transactional highlights.
- **Tertiary Cyan-Teal (`#0D9488`):** Deep support hue for interactive hover states, secondary metrics, and deep data visualizations.
- **Neutral Dark Slate (`#1E293B`):** The primary readability baseline for high-legibility typographic hierarchy, replacing harsh absolute black with an accessible slate tone.

### Surface & Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFB` provides a soothing, ultra-light cool-gray foundation that prevents eye strain during extended merchant inventory and ledger management.
- **Card & Sheet Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` pure white to separate elevated interactives from background canvas.
- **Border Subtle:** `#E2E8F0` for low-contrast structure, divider lines, and card containers.
- **B-CHAIN Verification Glow:** `rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.12)` subtle emerald-teal washes backing immutable transaction records and verified merchant credentials.
- **B-Market Commerce Tag:** `rgba(248, 173, 60, 0.15)` background with `#C27803` text for deals, flash promotions, and catalog spotlights.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric contours, open counters, and high legibility across compact mobile screens. It accommodates Indonesian financial nomenclature and dual-purpose catalog and transaction layouts.

### Hierarchy & Style Instructions
- **Brand & Impact (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Reserved for financial account balances, merchant revenue milestones, onboarding titles, and hero promotional highlights. Always set tight line heights with subtle negative letter-spacing for refined editorial polish.
- **Section & Product Titles (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Defines marketplace categories, wallet breakdown sections, and modal headers.
- **Body & Data Readouts (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Designed for high scannability in product descriptions, financing terms, and ledger timestamps. `body-md` is standard for standard interface reading.
- **Badges & Micro-meta (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Specifically tuned for verification pills, security checksum tags (B-CHAIN), and B-Market status badges with uppercase tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured **fluid grid** calibrated specifically for portrait mobile screens while flexing cleanly to tablet orientations.

### Rhythm & Grid
- **Mobile Width (default):** 4-column fluid layout with an edge margin of `1rem` (16px) and an inner column gutter of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Tablet / Large Handheld:** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Vertical Cadence:** A strict 4px/8px incremental scale rules internal component padding. Card padding defaults to `1rem` (`space-md`), escalating to `1.25rem` on prominent wallet or revenue banners.
- **Safe Ergonomic Touch Targets:** Interactive controls (action buttons, quantity counters, tab triggers) adhere to an absolute minimum touch zone of 48×48px.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting instead for **soft ambient diffusions and micro-tinted layers** that feel light, modern, and clean.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFB` surface. No shadow, purely structural.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white container `#FFFFFF` with a subtle boundary hairline `1px solid #F1F5F9` and an ultra-soft tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.03);`
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Navigation Bars):** Elevated floating elements (such as bottom sheets, checkout trays, or quick-action bars):
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.05);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Semi-opaque backdrop (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.45)`) combined with bottom sheet surfaces utilizing high-spread elevation.
- **B-CHAIN Trust Aura:** Verified ledger nodes or certificate cards incorporate an inner glow effect:
  `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.25), 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.08);`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, accessible architecture anchored by generous curves that soften enterprise banking into a supportive everyday tool.

- **Standard Cards & Modules:** `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem) for product cards, revenue panels, and feature groupings.
- **Interactive Controls:** `rounded-xl` (12px / 0.75rem) for form fields, input groups, and secondary actions.
- **Badges, Tags & Primary CTAs:** Full pill styling (`rounded-full`) for high-conversion action buttons, status indicators, and B-CHAIN authentication pills.
- **Image & Icon Containers:** Continuous squircle or smooth `rounded-xl` corner radii to echo modern mobile OS design paradigms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Teal Solid):** Background `#00A39D`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, height 48px, radius `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`. Pressed state shifts to `#0D9488` with a 0.98 scale transition.
- **Secondary / Accent Action (Orange Solid):** Background `#F8AD3C`, text `#FFFFFF`, height 48px. Used for high-priority marketing events, buy-now triggers, and catalog promotions.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #00A39D`, text `#00A39D`, transparent background. On tap, applies a `rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.06)` wash.

### Badges & Verification Tags
- **B-CHAIN Verified Badge:** Pill layout with a glowing emerald-teal checkmark. Container: `rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.1)`, border: `1px solid rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.3)`, text: `#00A39D` in `label-sm` uppercase. Features an optional micro hash indicator (e.g., `#B-9842F`).
- **B-Market Badge:** Pill container: `rgba(248, 173, 60, 0.14)`, text: `#D97706`, accompanied by the mini market-stall icon.
- **Halal & Syariah Compliance Stamp:** Crisp teal pill with subtle gold/saffron star detailing.

### Cards
- **Merchant Wallet & Overview Card:** Prominent teal-to-darker-teal gradient (`135deg, #00A39D 0%, #00817D 100%`) with white typography, gold/orange micro-accents, rounded-2xl (16px), and soft ambient teal shadow.
- **Product & Showcase Cards:** Clean `#FFFFFF` base, rounded-2xl, 1px border `#F1F5F9`, image container with `rounded-xl`, price displayed in bold `#1E293B` with secondary badge in `#F8AD3C`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Height 50px, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E293B`, rounded-xl (12px), horizontal padding `1rem`.
- **Focused State:** Border transitions cleanly to `#00A39D` with a subtle ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 163, 157, 0.12)`.
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F1F5F9` background, search icon in `#00A39D`, placeholder `#64748B`.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Transaction History List:** Dividers in `#F1F5F9`. Left-aligned avatar with curved square icon in teal or orange tint, center-aligned transaction title and B-CHAIN verified tag, right-aligned monetary balance in `#1E293B`.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating or docked container with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px top border `#E2E8F0`, active icon and text in `#00A39D`, inactive items in `#94A3B8`.
